CAPUCCINO

about us

Cappuccino is one of the most popular coffee drinks in the world. It is made from espresso, steamed milk, and a thick layer of milk foam. The perfect balance between strong coffee and creamy milk creates a rich and smooth flavor that coffee lovers enjoy every day. Originally from Italy, cappuccino has become a favorite choice in cafés around the globe. It is often served with cocoa powder or cinnamon on top, adding an extra touch of aroma and taste. Whether enjoyed in the morning or during a relaxing afternoon break, cappuccino offers a warm and comforting coffee experience.





ESSPRESO

about us


Espresso is the foundation of many coffee beverages and is known for its bold and intense flavor. It is prepared by forcing hot water through finely ground coffee beans under high pressure, producing a rich and concentrated shot of coffee. Originating in Italy, espresso is appreciated for its strong aroma, smooth texture, and golden layer of crema on top. It is perfect for those who enjoy the pure taste of coffee and need a quick boost of energy. Despite its small size, espresso delivers a powerful coffee experience that is loved by coffee enthusiasts worldwide.
